Vaqt - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Data Collected

Vaqt only uses the following data to calculate prayer times:

  • Location data: Your latitude and longitude coordinates, obtained through the browser's Geolocation API and only with your permission.
  • City search text: If you search for a city instead of sharing your location, the name you type.

How Data Is Used

This data is only used to:

  • Calculate prayer times for your location (via the Aladhan API)
  • Display your city name, or find the city you searched for (via OpenStreetMap Nominatim)

Data Storage

  • Your location and settings are stored only in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local).
  • Vaqt has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell or track anything. The only data that leaves your browser goes to the two services below, and only to answer your request.
  • All local data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the extension.

Third-Party Services

Vaqt sends requests to these services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Aladhan API (aladhan.com): for calculating prayer times. Your coordinates and the calculation method are sent.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org): for turning coordinates into a city name, and for city search. Your coordinates, or the city name you typed, are sent.
