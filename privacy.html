Vaqt - Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

Data Collected

Vaqt only uses the following data to calculate prayer times:

  • Location data: Your latitude and longitude coordinates, obtained through the browser's Geolocation API and only with your permission.

How Data Is Used

Your location data is only used to:

  • Calculate prayer times for your location (via the Aladhan API)
  • Display your city name (via OpenStreetMap Nominatim)

Data Storage

  • Your location data is stored only in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local).
  • No data is sent to external servers or shared with third parties.
  • All local data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the extension.

Third-Party Services

Vaqt uses the following third-party APIs:

  • Aladhan API: For calculating prayer times. Only coordinates and calculation method are sent.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: For resolving coordinates to a city name. Only coordinates are sent.
